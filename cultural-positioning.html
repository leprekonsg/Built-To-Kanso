01 / Two lanes

The school we work in is already environmental.

Form-School (形势派) reads the actual ground. Wind paths, water drainage, room geometry, sightlines, terrain. This is the lane Built-To-Kanso lives in. The metaphysical lane (Period-9 cycles, Bazi birth-charts, luopan rotation rings, colour-element astrology) is a different table altogether.

The metaphysical lane is not pictured to be argued with. It is pictured to mark the boundary. Built-To-Kanso never claims it has been refuted; it claims it has not been entered.

02 / The trap

Why “distilled wisdom, not age-old superstition” fails.

Three failure modes. Any one of them would lose the Cultural-mode homeowner; together they break the brand.

01

It mislabels the school you’re working in.

Form-School (形势派) is the environment-reading tradition. It is already empirical: wind, water, terrain, geometry, sightlines. The brief picks it precisely because it is not the metaphysical, Period-9, luopan-chart lane.

Saying “we distilled the wisdom from the superstition” tells the cultural reviewer that you don’t know which school you’re in. The etymology disclosure is non-negotiable for exactly this reason.

Form-School was never the superstition.
02

It re-centers the thing you’re inverting.

The Nanyang positioning works by being something else, not by arguing against mainland feng shui. “Wisdom, not superstition” makes the superstitious frame your product’s reference point.

Like a “real coffee” brand whose tagline is (unlike Starbucks): you’ve made the rival the brand’s job. Quietly being a wind-aware studio for HDB homes does the inversion without naming the rival.

Be something else. Don’t fight the lane.
03

It breaks the calm voice.

The voice asks rather than instructs. It reports as receipt, not patch note. It calibrates rather than oversells. It refuses beautifully.

“This is wisdom, not superstition” is argument copy. It defends. It picks a fight on a surface that should be observing the home, not relitigating the field.

The product reports. It does not defend.
03 / The right frame

Be the room with the louvres open.

The frame the brief already supports: cultural reading and physical reading, in the same room. Both ride the same physics.

The same instinct, with instruments.

Form-school always read the actual environment. The site visit, the wind direction, the slope of the land, the placement of doors. The instruments are new. The reading is not.

WebGPU LBM, plan-geometry.json, and Resonance Hours are the new instruments. They give the form-school reader something the masters never had: a 256x256 lattice solving the wind across the actual unit, in real time, against the actual outdoor weather.

That is continuity, not correction.

04 / Etymology

The disclosure that earns the credibility.

Three boundaries that must hold on every surface that names the tradition. Cultural-credibility critique is a real risk per PRODUCT.md, not a hypothetical.

Aesthetic register kansō ≠ kasō Kansō is the Japanese aesthetic of simplicity (remove rather than place). Kasō is house astrology by birth-year. We use the first; we do not practise the second.
School of feng shui Form-School (形势派) ≠ Compass / Period-9 Form-School reads the actual terrain, geometry, and water. Compass-School and its descendants chart by birth-element and 20-year cycles. We are in the first lane. The second is not on our table.
Geographic anchor 1.35° N ≠ 22°–40° N Singapore latitude inverts the classical south-facing rule. West-sun heat path (16:00–18:30) and NE/SW monsoon vectors are the rule, not the exception. If a rule does not pass through Singapore, it does not ship.

These three lines, in some form, must appear on every surface that names the tradition. They are not legalese. They are how a Singaporean homeowner forwards the demo to their family without losing face.

05 / Voice

How the calm voice actually sounds.

Four checks for any new copy on any new surface. If a line fails one of these, rewrite it before it ships.

01 — Asks
“The house is listening.”
not “Place the buffer here.”
02 — Reports
“Entry rush softened. One corner left empty.”
not “Successfully optimised airflow.”
03 — Calibrates
“You’re on a low floor; Resonance Hours will be quiet here.”
not “Premium results unlocked.”
04 — Refuses
“This wall is not asking to be changed.”
not “Error: invalid placement.”

The brand poster is normative: Calm by design. Structured by intent. Fluid in motion. Tropical by context. The trap-frame breaks the first two words. The right frame holds all four.
